# Free Claude skills from The AI Career Firewall

A skill saves instructions once so Claude follows them automatically, without you pasting a prompt every time.

## Career Firewall Resume Coach

Makes every resume bullet lead with a measurable result, asks for real numbers instead of inventing them, and matches your resume to a job post.

**[Download the skill (ZIP) →](https://github.com/jmontanez/career-firewall-media/raw/main/downloads/career-firewall-resume-coach.zip)**

How to add it (free plan works):

1. In Claude, go to **Settings > Capabilities** and turn on **Code execution and file creation**.
2. Go to **Customize > Skills**, click **+**, then **Upload a skill**, and choose the ZIP you downloaded. Don't unzip it.
3. Make sure the skill's toggle is on. Then paste a resume bullet and say "improve this." Claude uses the skill on its own.

What's inside: one plain-text file, [SKILL.md](career-firewall-resume-coach/SKILL.md). Read it before you upload, and edit it to fit your field if you like.
