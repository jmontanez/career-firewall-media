1. The quick mock interview prompt

Paste into any AI chat
You are my interview coach. I'm an experienced professional with a real interview coming up.

1. Ask me to paste the job post. Wait for it.
2. From it, pick the 3 questions I'm most likely to get, including one about why I'm leaving or a gap.
3. Ask them one at a time. After each answer, score it out of 10, tell me the one fix that matters most, and rewrite it in my own words. Never invent numbers or results; ask me for them.
4. At the end, tell me which answer to practice most.

Start with step 1.

2. Five answers, fill in the brackets

Why were you the one let go?
I don't know the full criteria, and I won't guess. What I do know is what I delivered: [result]. My manager there, [title], is one of my references and can speak to it.

Why it works: Calm and short. Handing them a reference ends the topic.

Tell me about this gap.
After my role was cut in [month], I took [time] to [search carefully + one concrete thing: a course, certification, project, freelance work or family]. I finished [result]. I'm ready to be back full time, and I've been choosy about fit rather than taking the first offer.

Why it works: No apology. One sentence on why, one on what you did, one on being ready.

Tell me about yourself.
I'm a [title] with [X] years in [field]. Most recently I [best result with a number]. Before that, [one earlier proof]. Now I'm looking for [what this role offers], which is why I applied here.

Why it works: Present, past proof, future. Under 90 seconds. Skip your childhood and first job.

Do you have AI experience?
Yes. I use AI every week for [task], and it saves me about [time]. I still check every output, because [risk in your field].

Why it works: Not true yet? Use the mock-interviewer prompt on your own job description and do one real task this week.

Tell me about a time you failed.
Situation: [one sentence]. My part: [what you owned]. What I did: [2-3 actions, including what you changed after]. Result: [what happened next, with a number if you have one].

Why it works: Pick a real failure from a few years ago that you fixed. The fix is the point.
