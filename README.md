## 34. 100 applications, no callbacks? Check the top third first

Paste your resume, then:

```
Read only the top third of my resume. In one sentence, what job do I seem to want, and what's my best result? If that's unclear, rewrite it.
```

Why it works: recruiters skim. If the top doesn't say what you want and what you've done, the rest never gets read.

## 33. Will AI replace software engineers? (Interview answer)

Employers surveyed by the World Economic Forum expect software developer jobs to grow by 2030. When an interviewer asks how you use AI, say: "It writes my first draft of code and tests. I spend my time on design, review, and the bugs it misses." Then turn your real work into resume bullets:

```
Here's what I worked on last month. Split it into tasks AI could draft and decisions only I made. Turn the decisions into 3 resume bullets with numbers. Ask me for any number you need.
```

Why it works: the decisions are the part of the job that's growing. Put them on your resume.

## 32. Job post asks for a skill you don't have? (Tool Drop: Coursera in ChatGPT)

Paste the job post and your resume into ChatGPT, then:

```
Here's a job post and my resume. Which skills does the job ask for that I'm missing? Rank them by how often the post mentions them.
```

Then start a new message with the word Coursera, for example: "Coursera, teach me the basics of SQL." It brings Coursera lesson videos into the chat. The full course still happens on Coursera.

## 31. "Why should we hire you if AI can do this job?" (Interview answer)

Say: "AI can draft the work. I decide what's right, catch what it gets wrong, and get people to act on it." Then give one real example. No example ready? Paste the job description and:

```
Read this job description. List the 3 decisions in this job AI can't make on its own. Then write me a 40-word answer to 'why hire you if AI can do this?' using one real example from my resume.
```

Why it works: you don't argue with AI. You show the judgment the job still needs, with proof.

## 30. Recruiter asks "Do you have AI experience?" (Interview answer)

Don't say no. Say: "I use AI every week for one part of my job, and it saves me about two hours." Not true yet? Paste your job description and:

```
Pick one task from this job I could do faster with AI this week. Give me the steps, then one sentence I can use to describe the result in an interview.
```

Why it works: do it once this week, and the answer is true.

## 28. Does ChatGPT search Indeed jobs now? (Tool Drop: ChatGPT's Indeed app)

## 23. Make a 7-day plan after a layoff (Layoff-proof, part 7)

Use it the day after a layoff, then this:

```
I was just laid off. Make a seven-day plan with one task a day: file for unemployment, set a budget, update my resume, and apply to ten jobs.
```

Why it works: one small task a day turns panic into progress, and the urgent paperwork comes first.

## 22. Cut your resume to one page

Paste your full resume, then this:

```
Cut my resume to one page. Keep the bullets with numbers, drop jobs older than ten years, and put my three strongest skills at the top.
```

Why it works: recruiters skim fast, so the page should only hold results and the skills they're searching for.

## 21. Know what to ask HR if layoffs hit (Layoff-proof, part 6)

Before any meeting about cuts, use this:

```
I may be laid off. List the 10 questions I should ask HR about severance, health insurance, unused vacation, stock and references, most important first.
```

Why it works: people forget their questions in a stressful meeting; a ranked list makes sure you ask about money and coverage first.

Write a polite reply to my manager that says yes to the most important task, asks which of my other tasks can wait, and suggests a new deadline. Under 80 words.

Why it works: it says yes, shows your real workload, and hands the priority call back to your manager.

## 5. Negotiate a layoff package

Paste your severance letter (remove names and anything confidential), then this:

```
List every term I could negotiate, what to ask for, and draft a polite email asking for it.
```

Why it works: most people sign without asking about extra weeks of pay, health coverage, a prorated bonus, or a written reference. Not legal advice; check any agreement with HR or an employment lawyer.

## 4. Answer a last-minute "need this by tomorrow" request

Paste your boss's message, then this:

```
Write a short, friendly reply to this request. Confirm what's due and when, offer two scope options (a quick version by morning or a full version by end of day), and ask which matters most.
```

Why it works: your boss picks the scope, so you don't lose your night guessing.

## 3. Fix your weakest resume bullet

Paste one weak bullet from your resume, then this:

```
Rewrite this as one resume bullet that shows an outcome with a number. If you need a number I didn't give you, ask me for it. Don't make one up.
```

Why it works: it asks you for real numbers instead of inventing them. Real numbers are what get interviews.

```
Turn these notes into a status report with 3 sections: Done, At Risk, Decisions Needed. Under 150 words. Flag anything with no owner or date.

Paste your job description (or a public posting for your role), then this:

```
